Mr. Vijay Kapoor has a legacy of four decades for creating components for the automotive industry in India. For his invaluable commitment, dedication, and contribution towards the society, he has been honoured with several awards.

How the journey started

The story of the E-Rickshaw is connected to the pledge taken way back at IIT in 1972 — "to give back to the society". A paddle rickshaw ride of Vijay Kapoor from Chandni Chowk car parking to market exhibited the pain that a driver goes through every day. After a thought provoking conversation with the driver, Vijay Kapoor created a vehicle which is eco friendly, had a low running cost and requires almost zero human effort. And that is what led to the birth of the E-Rickshaw in India.

With a constant belief to create self employment for thousands of people, we want to upgrade their status in society by providing them with a more steady source of income.
