About the manufacturer

Saera Electric Auto Pvt. Ltd.

Mayuri is the flagship electric three-wheeler brand of Saera Electric Auto Pvt. Ltd. (SEAPL), part of the Satra Group of Companies. Commenced in 2011, Saera is an ISO 9001:2015 certified company and was the first to introduce e-rickshaws in India — and the first to receive a Vehicle Approval Certificate from the International Centre for Automotive Technology (ICAT), Manesar.

Saera manufactures cleaner, greener electric three-wheelers (passenger e-rickshaws and cargo e-loaders), electric low-speed two-wheelers (Yogo Bikes), and electric golf carts — with a production capacity of 60,000 electric three-wheelers and 2 lakh electric two-wheelers per year.
