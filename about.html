About Us

Where Vision Meets Motion

At Saera, we are more than a company — we are a group of brands united by a single purpose: to move people and goods cleanly, reliably and with dignity. Three brands, one commitment, and a shared belief that the way India moves should be quiet, efficient and built to last.

Vision

We aim to become one of the leading Electric Vehicle companies by elevating the level of automotive technology.

Mission

We are dedicated to accepting challenges of making and reaching goals in India with quality, worthwhile customer satisfaction.

Value

Integrity, commitment and innovation are our core values, and they make us an outstanding performer in the industry.

Why Saera?

Built on three of India's most trusted electric vehicle brands.

Our Craft, Your Journey

We don't just make products — we create vehicles that are lived in every day. Every detail, from the fit of a panel to the finish of a dashboard, is decided with care.

Every component, every feature, every hour behind it exists so your journey feels effortless.

Driven by People, Fueled by Passion

Saera is built because of our people — engineers, technicians and the teams on the line who bring precision and care to every vehicle that leaves the floor.

We are proud of the skill and the effort behind every machine we build.

Advancing Sustainable Futures

Sustainability is not a slogan — it is a design constraint we hold ourselves to, from the materials we choose to the emissions we never release.

From every efficient design to responsible manufacturing choices, we aim to make a cleaner, quieter future.

Looking Ahead

We're already ahead of many of the roads we are working on, exploring new ways to make the electric drivetrain smarter, lighter and further reaching.

Every step we take towards a wider, better-connected, more efficient future is a step we take together.

A Legacy of Innovation

For more than a decade, Saera has believed in the design of a driving culture built on experience — people first, and the technology that serves them second.
